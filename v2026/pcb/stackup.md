# PCB Stackup Target

## Main board

8-layer target:

1. Top signal/component
2. Ground
3. High-speed signal
4. Power
5. Ground
6. High-speed signal
7. Ground
8. Bottom signal/component

The final dielectric thickness and copper weights must be selected with the PCB fabricator so
PCIe/USB4/display/Ethernet impedance targets are achieved.

## Front panel

4-layer board is sufficient for LEDs, switches and low-speed control.

## Backplane

4–6 layers depending on the S-100 electrical requirements and connector strategy.

## Power board

4-layer power-oriented design with copper weight sized from measured load and transient
requirements.
